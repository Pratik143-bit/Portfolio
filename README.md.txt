### 1. Virat Kohli Score Analysis Dashboard

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Power BI
**Dataset:** Sample Score (Kaggle), 539 rows and 21 columns, 2008 to 2022

**Problem statement:** The objective of this project is to analyse Virat Kohli’s cricket performance across different opponents, matches, and years using data analytics and visualization techniques. The project aims to identify scoring patterns, evaluate batting performance, compare performances against different opponents, and understand trends in his career. An interactive Power BI dashboard is developed to present key performance indicators and insights that support effective and data-driven performance analysis.

**Approach:**
1.Data Collection: Collected cricket performance data related to Virat Kohli’s matches, runs, opponents, and yearly performance.
2.Data Cleaning and Preprocessing: Used Python and Pandas to clean the dataset, handle missing or inconsistent values, and prepare the data for analysis.
3.Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA): Examined match statistics, total runs, highest score, batting average, and performance against different opponents.
4.Data Visualization: Created charts and graphs to analyze runs by opponent, matches played against opponents, and yearly scoring trends.
5.Dashboard Development: Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard displaying KPIs and visualizations for a clear understanding of Virat Kohli’s career performance.
6.Insight Generation: Interpreted the visualizations to identify scoring trends, opponent-wise differences, and changes in yearly performance.


**Key findings:**
-Total Matches: The dashboard indicates a total of 516 matches analyzed, reflecting Virat Kohli's comprehensive match experience in the dataset. --Total Runs: He has scored approximately 24,000 runs, underscoring his significant contribution to the team's scoring. 
-Highest Score: Kohli's highest score is recorded at 254. 
-Batting Average: His batting average stands at 45.95. 
-Opponent-wise Runs: Among the opponents listed, Australia has the highest total runs, while South Africa has the lowest. 
-Matches by Opponent: England features the largest share of matches represented in the donut chart. 
-Yearly Performance: The visualization of yearly runs from 2008 to 2022 highlights fluctuations in Kohli's scoring performance across the years.


**Recommendation:**
 1. Analyze performance against opponents by comparing runs and batting averages. 
2. Track yearly trends to identify scoring fluctuations. 
3. Include advanced metrics like centuries and strike rates for a comprehensive overview. 
4. Implement filters for match formats, year, opponents, and venues for detailed analysis. 
5. Compare batting performance across formats to understand scoring differences. 
6. Enhance the dashboard with tooltips and detailed performance cards for better exploration of statistics.

**Tools:** Python (Pandas), SQL, Power BI, DAX

**Problem statement:** 
1. **Sales Data Analysis**: The manual analysis of extensive e-commerce sales data is characterized as both time-consuming and complex.

2. **Business Performance**: A robust understanding of key metrics such as revenue, profit, and product performance is crucial for assessing business growth effectively.

3. **Data-Driven Decisions**: The primary goal of this project is to leverage SQL and Power BI for sales data analysis, with the aim of uncovering trends that will facilitate improved business decision-making.


**Approach**
1. **Data Collection**: The initial phase involved gathering and meticulously examining e-commerce sales data to identify its structure and significant attributes, which is critical for foundational understanding.

2. **Data Analysis using SQL**: This step employed SQL queries to effectively filter, group, and summarize the sales data, enabling a deeper analysis that yields meaningful insights regarding sales trends and patterns.

3. **Dashboard Development**: Leveraging the analyzed data, an interactive Power BI dashboard was developed. This dashboard integrated KPI cards, various charts, and visualizations that facilitate easy interpretation and presentation of data insights.

4. **Performance Analysis**: The final phase focused on a comprehensive performance analysis, assessing critical metrics such as revenue, profit levels, monthly sales trends, and performance segmented by product and category. This analysis is essential for strategic decision-making based on data-driven insights. 


**Key findings**
1. **Revenue and Profit**: The evaluation focused on the overall sales revenue and profit to gauge the business's performance. A detailed analysis was conducted to ascertain profitability levels in relation to total revenue generated.

2. **Monthly Sales Trends**: An examination of changes in sales was undertaken across various months, revealing patterns and fluctuations in sales figures over time, which provides insights into seasonal impacts and operational efficiencies.

3. **Product Performance**: A comparative analysis of sales was performed on a product-wise basis, highlighting the contributions of individual products to total sales. This allowed for the identification of high-performing products versus those that may require strategic adjustments.

4. **Category Analysis**: A category-wise sales analysis was completed to determine how different product categories affect overall sales. This analysis aims to identify which categories are driving sales growth and which are lagging, offering a comprehensive view of the business's product portfolio performance.

**Recommendations**

2. **Improve Product Planning:** Conducting a detailed product-wise analysis helps in identifying which products are performing well and which are not. This insight aids in making informed decisions regarding product development, marketing, and allocation of resources.

3. **Sales Forecasting:** Historical monthly sales data should be utilized to forecast future sales. This process supports effective planning for inventory management and helps in ensuring that supply meets anticipated demand.

4. **Dashboard Enhancement:** To facilitate deeper insights into business performance, it is recommended to expand the dashboard to include additional key performance indicators (KPIs) such as profit margin, average order value, and total orders. This comprehensive view supports better analysis and decision-making.

### 3. Diamond Price Prediction

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn

**Problem statement:** 
1. **Price Estimation Challenges**: The valuation of diamonds is intrinsically complex due to the influence of key characteristics like carat weight, cut quality, colour grade, and clarity, which collectively contribute to the variability and difficulty in accurately estimating their market prices.

2. **Manual Valuation Limitations**: The traditional approach to manually estimate diamond prices is often labour-intensive and prone to inconsistencies, leading to potential inaccuracies in valuation assessments.

3. **Proposed Machine Learning Solution**: To address these challenges, the project proposes the development of a Linear Regression model that leverages both diamond characteristics and historical pricing data. This model will aim to provide reliable predictions of diamond prices, offering a more efficient and consistent alternative to manual estimation methods.


**Approach**
1. **Data Collection:** A diamond dataset was utilized, encompassing attributes such as carat, cut, colour, clarity, depth, table, and dimensions (x, y, z).

2. **Data Preprocessing:** The dataset was inspected and prepared for model development using Python and Pandas, addressing data quality issues.

3. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Relationships between diamond characteristics and prices were examined through statistical summaries and visual visualizations.

4. **Feature Selection:** Key numerical and categorical features relevant to predicting diamond prices were identified.

5. **Model Development:** A Linear Regression model was created with Python and Scikit-learn to estimate prices based on the chosen features.

6. **Model Evaluation:** The model was assessed using appropriate regression metrics to evaluate its predictive performance.


**Key findings**
1. **Carat Weight**: The carat weight of a diamond is a significant factor in determining its price. Larger diamonds typically command higher prices, assuming that other quality characteristics such as cut, colour, and clarity remain constant.

2. **Cut, Colour, and Clarity**: These attributes are integral to the overall quality of diamonds and are crucial in differentiating their prices in the market. A diamond's cut influences its brilliance and overall visual appeal, while colour refers to the presence of any hue, and clarity assesses the presence of internal or external imperfections.

3. **Diamond Dimensions**: The physical dimensions of a diamond—often measured in terms of x (width), y (depth), and z (height)—are vital metrics that provide insight into the diamond's size and can play a role in predicting its market value.

4. **Feature Relationships**: Through exploratory data analysis, one can uncover the correlations between various diamond characteristics and their prices, revealing how factors might interplay to influence value.

5. **Prediction Performance**: Utilizing a Linear Regression model enables the identification of underlying patterns in historical data, allowing for the estimation of optimal prices for diamonds with specific features. This approach leverages the relationship between different characteristics to forecast potential market values effectively.


**Recommendation: **
1. **Improve Data Quality**: The initial step emphasizes the importance of ensuring high-quality data by checking for missing values, eliminating duplicates, and verifying that dimension values are realistic prior to training the model.

2. **Feature Engineering**: This stage involves the exploration of additional features that could enhance model performance, as well as suitable encoding methods for categorical variables to facilitate effective analysis.

3. **Model Comparison**: The document suggests comparing various algorithms, specifically Linear Regression, with other models like Decision Trees, Random Forests, and Gradient Boosting. This comparative analysis is crucial for determining which model yields the best predictive improvements.

4. **Model Evaluation**: To assess the effectiveness of the different models, various metrics such as R-squared (R²), Mean Absolute Error (MAE), and Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) should be employed. These metrics provide insights into the model's prediction accuracy and the extent of error.

5. **Deployment**: Finally, the document proposes developing a user-friendly Streamlit web application. This application would allow users to input specific characteristics of diamonds and receive real-time predictions regarding their price.

Overall, the approach combines rigorous data preparation, innovative feature development, comparative modelling, robust evaluation, and practical deployment, aimed at creating an effective diamond price prediction tool.
